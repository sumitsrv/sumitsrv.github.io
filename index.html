Welcome! If you are here, I thank you—and applaud you—for being interested in the real me. The LinkedIn version is not a falsehood, but it does not really show who I am. What I love, what I cannot stand, what inspires me, what made me the person I am today. Not one thing. Not one person.

I love research. I love engineering. Curiosity is my thing. But above all, the best of humanity—rather, every living being—is my sole motivator.

There is so much more to talk about. It will come sooner than later. And yes—I love food. Everyone should. It is such a beautiful thing—to make it, smell it, share it.
